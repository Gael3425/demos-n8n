1 · Abandoned cart recovery

1:58 · 1080p

The case almost nobody shows: when the API fails

Full flow

Three entry points (cart abandoned, purchase completed, and an hourly scheduled job), a state machine and an audit table. Everything split into modules and documented inside the workflow itself.

  • Real idempotency: the same event is sent twice and creates no new row and no new reminder.
  • Email API failure: three attempts with backoff, the state does not advance, and the last error is stored with the original message.
  • Re-running is safe: the scheduler runs again and the numbers do not change.
  • Recovery: if the customer buys, the webhook stops the sequence and no further message goes out.

2 · Lead capture and PDF invoices to Excel

1:28 · 1080p

Two back-office flows, with the odd cases handled

Form + PDF

A public form that delivers into the CRM and a PDF-to-Excel invoice converter. Both with validation, deduplication and delivery with retries.

  • Incomplete lead: it is not lost, it goes to review and the team is notified.
  • Duplicate lead: detected by fingerprint before writing; no duplicate rows or alerts.
  • Invoice: extracts the text, checks that subtotal + tax matches the total, deduplicates by invoice number.
  • Unreadable PDF: goes to review with the reason. Numbers are never invented.
